Quick start

  1. Pick a background, or upload your own picture.
  2. Add characters, props, text and speech bubbles.
  3. Drag them on the stage. Press ◆ Keyframe at different times to animate.
  4. Use New scene in the scenes strip to build a story, then ▶ Play movie.
  5. Add music or record your voice, then Export MP4 (or WebM).
  6. Sit: select a character and press 🪑 Sit — it walks to the nearest free seat (chair, sofa, bench, bed, table or shape). Pick a specific seat in the Inspector.
  7. Cars: select a character, tap 🚗 Car, then select more characters and press “Get in nearest vehicle”.

Shortcuts: K keyframe · Home/End jump · Ctrl+D duplicate · wheel = camera zoom · drag background to pan.
